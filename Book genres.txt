Book genres are categories that classify literature based on thematic, stylistic, and narrative similarities, helping readers find preferred stories and assisting in marketing. Core fiction genres include romance, fantasy, mystery, thriller, science fiction, historical, horror, and action-adventure, along with nonfiction categories like memoir, biography, and self-help.

Primary Fiction Genres
Romance: Focuses on relationships and emotional connections, typically requiring a happy ending.
Fantasy: Features magical worlds, supernatural elements, and often epic, heroic endeavors.
Science Fiction (Sci-Fi): Based on futuristic technology, space exploration, or scientific advancements.
Mystery/Crime: Centers on solving a crime, such as a murder or disappearance, through clues.
Thriller/Suspense: Features high-stakes danger and fast-paced narratives designed to keep readers on edge.
Historical Fiction: Stories set in the past, often incorporating real historical events or settings.
Horror: Designed to provoke fear, shock, and suspense, often using supernatural or psychological elements.
Action/Adventure: Focuses on action-packed, fast-moving plots, such as journeys or treasure hunts.

Non-Fiction Genres
Memoir & Biography: Personal accounts of life experiences, with memoirs focusing on specific periods and biographies on a person's entire life.
Self-Help: Books aimed at helping readers improve specific areas of their lives.
True Crime: A non-fiction examination of real-life crimes.

Key Age Categories/Categorizations
Young Adult (YA): Geared toward readers aged 12 to 18, often exploring themes of identity and first love.
Middle Grade (MG): Targeted at readers aged 8 to 12, focusing on younger protagonists.
Literary Fiction: Focuses on character development and thematic depth rather than plot-driven genre conventions.
Contemporary Fiction: Stories set in modern times.
